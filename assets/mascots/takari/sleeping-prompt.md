Image 2 is ONLY a broad reference for sitting cross-legged with both hands relaxed on the lap, NOT a character/style reference. Create a NEW single original pixel-art sprite of Takari sitting cross-legged on the ground and quietly SLEEPING, eyes fully closed, long dragon snout tipped gently downward, relaxed friendly peaceful expression. Same ochre-gold skin, gray curved horns and head spikes, pale yellow belly scales, folded pale yellow dragon wings, white claws, dark teal polo with white collar and sleeve trim and tiny white university emblem shape, curled arrow-tipped tail around the seated feet. Exactly two arms and two hands resting together on the lap; exactly two folded/crossed dragon legs. Full body entirely visible, generous transparent margins especially above the head for animated sleep letters added by the app later. Three-quarter orientation facing slightly left, compact grounded seated silhouette, no chair. Keep original Takari identity rather than copying the human. Remove mango, book, trophy and fire; NO objects in hands. Match chunky crisp detailed pixel-art treatment and outlined flat colors of Image 1, not vector art and not realistic. One sprite only, actual transparent background with alpha, no floor or ground shadow, no scene, no caption or lettering or Z symbols, no watermarks, no multiple poses. Do not reproduce any watermark, text or diagonal lines from the pose reference.
